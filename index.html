Our Story

A Bakery Born from Passion,
in the Hills of Yercaud

Tucked away in the misty embrace of the Shevaroy Hills, VB The Bakery is more than just a bakery — it's a warm corner of happiness. Every loaf, every cake, and every cookie is crafted by hand with the finest ingredients, baked fresh each morning as the Yercaud hills wake up.

We believe in slow baking, honest flavours, and the kind of warmth you feel the moment you walk through our door. Whether you're a tourist discovering our hills for the first time or a local who calls Yercaud home — there's always a seat and a fresh pastry waiting for you.
